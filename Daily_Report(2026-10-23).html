🌾 Wheat Commentary

  • CHICAGO, Oct 22 (Reuters) - Chicago Board of Trade wheat dropped on Thursday to near three-month lows hit the previous day on hopes for the result of diplomatic talks between Russia and Ukraine.

    • Chicago wheat fell on Thursday on the prospect of diplomatic talks bringing de-escalation in Russia's war with Ukraine and its damage to shipping. GRA/

    • Russian President Vladimir Putin hosted US special envoy Steve Witkoff to review new ideas on ending the war, after Witkoff held talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy.

    • Attacks continued on Thursday, with Russia pounding Kyiv with ballistic missiles and Ukraine sending hundreds of drones towards Moscow overnight.

    • Rains in the US southwest Plains were expected to aid the hard red winter wheat crop in western Kansas, Texas and Oklahoma, pushing Kansas City wheat futures lower, according to analysts.

    • The US Department of Agriculture reported weekly wheat export sales of 239,600 metric tons in the current marketing year.

    • CBOT December soft red winter wheat WZ26 closed 3-1/2 cents lower at $6.24-3/4 per bushel, near the three-month low hit on Wednesday.

    • K.C. December hard red winter wheat KWZ26 settled 7-1/4 cents lower at $6.65-3/4 a bushel, having reached fresh three-month lows.

    • Minneapolis December spring wheat MWEZ26 ended 9-3/4 cents lower at $6.41-3/4 a bushel, its lowest point since July 2.

🌽 Corn Commentary

  • CHICAGO, Oct 22 (Reuters) - Chicago Board of Trade corn futures weakened Thursday after hitting three-month lows the previous day, as diplomatic talks increased hopes for de-escalation in the Russian war on Ukraine and the harvest advanced in the US Midwest.

    • Dry weather conditions will be beneficial for harvesting in the US Midwest through early next week, according to Commodity Weather Group.

    • Russian President Vladimir Putin hosted US envoy Steve Witkoff on Wednesday, a day after Witkoff met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy in Norway, raising hopes for de-escalation in Black Sea warfare that has impacted critical grain shipping routes.

    • The USDA reported net export sales of US corn in the week ended October 15 at 1,527,100 metric tons, above a range of trade expectations for 700,000 to 1,500,000 tons.

    • Higher crude oil futures CLc1 lent support, reflecting corn's use as a feedstock for ethanol.

    • CBOT December corn CZ26 settled 2-3/4 cents lower at $4.60-1/2 a bushel.


    (Reporting by Renee Hickman; Editing by Cynthia Osterman)

    (( renee.hickman@thomsonreuters.com ))

🌱 Soybean Commentary

  • CHICAGO, Oct 22 (Reuters) - Chicago Board of Trade soybean futures rose on Thursday as weekly export sales came in at the higher end of trade expectations, offsetting fading harvest pressure.

    • The USDA reported net export sales of US soybeans in the week ended October 15 at 2,841,200 metric tons, up noticeably from the prior four-week average but in line with trade expectations for 1,800,000 to 3,000,000 tons.

    • Drier weather in the Midwest this week accelerated harvesting of what the government projects to be bumper corn and soybean crops, but analysts said much of the pressure from that harvest is beginning to fade as it gets closer to completion.

    • CBOT November soybeans SX26 closed 7-3/4 cents higher at $12.53 per bushel.

    • CBOT December soymeal SMZ26 was $1.00 lower at $347.30 per ton.

    • CBOT December soyoil BOZ26 rose 0.77 cent to settle at 66.74 cents per pound.


    (Reporting by Renee Hickman; Editing by Will Dunham)

🛢 Vegetable Oil Commentary

  • Update for market close

    JAKARTA, Oct 22 (Reuters) - Malaysian palm oil futures rose on Thursday, buoyed by gains in rival soyoil on the Dalian and Chicago exchanges, and stronger crude oil prices.

    The benchmark palm oil contract FCPOc3 for January delivery on the Bursa Malaysia Derivatives Exchange gained 26 ringgit, or 0.55%, to 4,793 ringgit ($1,174.47) a metric ton at the close.

    Brent prices rose to their highest since May on Thursday after more Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia stoked concerns over a broader escalation of conflict and further disruptions to crude oil supplies from the world's key exporting region.O/R

    Stronger crude oil futures make palm a more attractive option for biodiesel feedstock.

    Dalian's most-active soyoil contract DBYcv1 rose 0.59%, while its palm oil contract DCPcv1 dropped 0.6%. Soyoil prices on the Chicago Board of Trade BOcv1 were up 0.53%.

    Palm oil tracks rival edible oils, as it competes for a share of the global vegetable oils market.

    Cargo surveyors estimated that Malaysian palm oil products exports for October 1-20 rose between 58.5% and 78.4% from a month earlier.

    Crude palm oil prices are expected to stay above 4,600 ringgit ($1,128) per metric ton in November on stronger export demand, sustained biodiesel consumption and a seasonal decline in production, the Malaysian Palm Oil Council said on Thursday.

    The ringgit MYR=, palm's currency of trade, weakened 0.06% against the dollar, making the commodity slightly cheaper for buyers holding foreign currencies.

    ($1 = 4.0810 ringgit)
